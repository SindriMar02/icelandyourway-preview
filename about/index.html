About Iceland Your Way

What

Iceland Your Way we are here to honestly accompany you in creating your perfect Icelandic experience. Your requirements coupled with our experience and passion will give birth to the ideal journey.

Our expert team continually seeks out new and captivating destinations across Iceland and is always keen to take on challenging projects and expeditions . We offer a wide array of possibilities for your remarkable journey. Iceland your way ´s name was chosen intentionally. Name your idea , together we will make it happen!

Who

From two passionate trekkers, we have expanded into a multi-cultural team of 28 skilled members deeply committed to the island we call home. Our approach enable us deliver the best level of service you expect when cooperating with a local agency .

Flexible and open-minded , our different backgrounds and perspectives spark fresh ideas ,all focus on one aim showing the best of Iceland to you !

How

For 25 years , we have developed an extensive network of trustworthy partners to provide you quality services and "off the beaten track" knowledge .

Our on-site equipment, food and logistics center makes us the top choice for tailor-made holidays and specialised groups or expeditions . This offers flexibility and provides us with all the equipment to efficiently arrange any type of adventure and activity.
